# Chen Jiang

Chen Jiang is a Global Artificial Intelligence Solutions Analyst at the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in Copenhagen. His work spans AI systems, software and systems engineering, and technical delivery, including generative AI, retrieval and agentic systems, automation, AI security, and edge and local AI.

At UNDP, Chen combines hands-on engineering with technical coordination across global infrastructure and Country Office work. He works across architecture, software implementation, integration, evaluation, deployment and live operations, translating requirements into technical work and navigating the dependencies between applications, data, infrastructure and security.

Before joining UNDP, he worked at Dartmouth on autonomous systems and image-based navigation in GPS-denied environments, co-designed the system architecture, built the software and simulation environment, and was first author of a paper presented at the 2023 Pan Pacific Microelectronics Symposium. Earlier, he founded and operated businesses in Australia and worked in media and communication in China and Australia.
